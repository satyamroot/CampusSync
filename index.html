How CampusSync Works

Straightforward 3-step workflow built for everyday student discovery.

📝

1. Report or Browse

Log a lost or found item with colour and location, or search through open spaces and upcoming club workshops.

⚡

2. Smart Match

Our rule-based engine scores Category (+3), Colour (+2), Location (+2), and Keywords (+1) to highlight possible matches.

🤝

3. Connect & Reclaim

Verify items safely with finders, bookmark quiet study spots, or register interest for the next campus competition.
