⚡ Rule-Based Matching

CampusSync automatically compares your report against opposite submissions (Lost against Found) using a deterministic scoring formula:

  • Same Category +3 pts
  • Same Colour +2 pts
  • Same Location +2 pts
  • Similar Description +1 pt
Max Score: 8/8 — Scores ≥ 4 are highlighted on the Smart Match Radar as "Possible Match".

🛡️ Privacy Guarantee

No student personal email is ever exposed to the public web. In this prototype, submissions are stored in your browser session. Matches are always presented cautiously as "Possible Match" so finders and owners verify in person.
